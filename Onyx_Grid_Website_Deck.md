## 0 · DESIGN BRIEF (not a page section — guidance for the builder)

**Theme:** Light throughout. No dark hero. Business / enterprise-consulting outlook — think a boutique cloud & AI engineering practice, not a defence vendor.

**Palette**
- Background: `#FAFAF7` (warm off-white, not stark white)
- Primary accent: `#4A6FA5` (muted steel/slate blue — trust, cloud, fintech)
- Secondary accent: `#7C9CBF` (lighter blue, for hover states / secondary CTAs)
- Text primary: `#1E2328`
- Text secondary: `#5B6470`
- Success/stat highlight: `#3B8266` (muted teal-green, used sparingly for metrics)
- Borders/dividers: `#E4E1D8`

*Rationale: pale blue reads as enterprise/trustworthy across cloud, fintech, and security — and sits as a lighter sibling to Tervox Grid's dark tactical cyan, without competing with it.*

**Typography:** Clean sans-serif for headings (e.g. Inter, Söhne, or similar geometric grotesque), slightly warmer serif or humanist sans for body copy if a second family is wanted. Avoid anything that reads "tactical" or monospace-heavy — save monospace strictly for stack/tech tags.

**Tone:** Confident, understated, senior. Short sentences. No hype adjectives. Let specifics (numbers, named technologies, engagement models) carry the credibility instead of superlatives.

**Logo/name lockup:** "ONYX GRID" wordmark, no tactical iconography. Consider a simple geometric mark (hexagon or interlocking node motif) rather than anything crosshair/radar-like — keep visual distance from Tervox.

## 2 · THE CHALLENGE

## Most software problems aren't technical — they're architectural

Teams don't fail because they lack good engineers. They fail because nobody owns the system end to end.

🧩 **Fragmented Ownership**
Cloud, security, and AI initiatives run as separate workstreams with separate vendors — nobody is accountable for how they fit together.

🐌 **Stalled AI Adoption**
Pilots and PoCs pile up. Few make it to production because nobody designed for reliability, cost, and governance from day one.

🔓 **Security Bolted On Late**
Security and compliance get treated as a review gate at the end, not a design constraint from the start — expensive to fix, easy to avoid.

💸 **Cloud Spend Without Cloud Discipline**
Migrations ship, but nobody owns cost, right-sizing, or architecture evolution afterward — the bill quietly grows every quarter.
